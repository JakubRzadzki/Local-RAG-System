NOVATECH LTD. EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (sample document, fictional company)

1. Working hours and remote work

The standard working day at NovaTech is 8 hours, Monday to Friday. Employees may
start work between 7:00 and 10:00. Core hours are 10:00-15:00, when everyone should
be available to their team.

Remote work is available up to three days a week, as agreed with your manager.
The company pays a flat allowance of 40 EUR per month towards internet and electricity
costs for people who work remotely at least eight days a month.

2. Vacation

The annual vacation entitlement is 20 days, rising to 26 days after 10 years of
service. In addition, every employee receives 2 "wellbeing days" per year that
require no explanation. Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR system at least
7 days in advance, except for short-notice leave (at most 4 days per year).

3. Equipment and security

Every new employee receives a laptop (macOS or Linux, your choice), a monitor and a
headset. Laptop disks must be encrypted, and two-factor authentication (2FA) is
mandatory for all company services. Lost equipment must be reported to the IT team
within 24 hours at it-help@novatech.example.

4. Training budget

Every employee has an annual training budget of 1,000 EUR and 3 days of paid training
leave. The budget covers courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll
over to the next year.

5. Reviews and bonuses

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. The annual bonus
ranges from 0 to 15% of annual salary and depends on team results and individual
performance. The bonus is paid in March of the following year.
